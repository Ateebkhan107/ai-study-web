## JEE Main 20 July 2021 Shift 2 Question 13

### Question 13

Maths · Differential Equations · Single correct

Let $y = y(x)$ satisfies the equation $\frac{dy}{dx} - \left| A \right| = 0$, for all $x > 0$, where $$A = \begin{bmatrix} y & \sin x & 1 \\ 0 & -1 & 1 \\ 2 & 0 & \frac{1}{x} \end{bmatrix}$$. If $y(\pi) = \pi + 2$, then the value of $y\left(\frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ is:

1.   $\frac{\pi}{2} + \frac{4}{\pi}$
2.   $\frac{\pi}{2} - \frac{1}{\pi}$
3.   $\frac{3\pi}{2} - \frac{1}{\pi}$
4.   $\frac{\pi}{2} - \frac{4}{\pi}$
