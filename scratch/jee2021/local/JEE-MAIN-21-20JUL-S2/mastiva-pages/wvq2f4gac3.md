### Question 66

Chemistry · Equilibrium · Single correct

A solution is $0.1\,\mathrm{M}$ in $\mathrm{Cl^-}$ and $0.001\,\mathrm{M}$ in $\mathrm{CrO_4^{2-}}$. Solid $\mathrm{AgNO_3}$ is gradually added to it. Assuming that the addition does not change the volume and $K_{sp}(\mathrm{AgCl})=1.7\times10^{-10}\,\mathrm{M^2}$ and $K_{sp}(\mathrm{Ag_2CrO_4})=1.9\times10^{-12}\,\mathrm{M^3}$, select the correct statement from the following:

1.   AgCl precipitates first because its $K_{sp}$ is high.
2.   Ag_2CrO_4 precipitates first as its $K_{sp}$ is low.
3.   $Ag_2CrO_4$ precipitates first because the amount of $Ag^+$ needed is low.
4.   AgCl will precipitate first as the amount of $Ag^+$ needed to precipitate is low.
