1.   $(3x - y)^2 + (x - 3y) + 2 = 0$
2.   $2(3x - y)^2 + (x - 3y) + 2 = 0$
3.   $(3x - y)^2 + 2(x - 3y) + 2 = 0$
4.   $2(x - 3y)^2 + (3x - y) + 2 = 0$
