### Question 52

Physics · Electromagnetic Waves · Numerical

If the radiation pressure exerted by the E.M. wave on the surface during a 10 minute exposure is $\frac{x}{10^9} \, \frac{\mathrm{N}}{\mathrm{m}^2}$. Find the value of $x$.
