## JEE Main 26 August 2021 Shift 1 Question 16

### Question 16

Maths · Matrices · Single correct

If $A = \begin{pmatrix} \frac{1}{\sqrt{5}} & \frac{2}{\sqrt{5}} \\ -\frac{2}{\sqrt{5}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{5}} \end{pmatrix}$, $B = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ i & 1 \end{pmatrix}$, $i = \sqrt{-1}$, and $Q = A^T BA$, then the inverse of the matrix $A Q^{2021} A^T$ is equal to :

1.   $\begin{pmatrix} \frac{1}{\sqrt{5}} & -2021 \\ 2021 & \frac{1}{\sqrt{5}} \end{pmatrix}$
2.   $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ -2021i & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
3.   $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 2021i & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
4.   $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & -2021i \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
