There are 50 divisions on the circular scale, and the main scale moves by 0.5 mm on a complete rotation. For a particular observation the reading on the main scale is 5 mm and the $20^{\text{th}}$ division of the circular scale coincides with reference line. Calculate the true reading.

2.   5.25\,$\mathrm{mm}$
3.   5.15\,$\mathrm{mm}$
4.   5.20\,$\mathrm{mm}$
