Physics · Magnetism and Matter · Single correct

Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

1.   $(C)$ and $(E)$ only
2.   $(B)$ and $(D)$ only
3.   $(A)$ and $(B)$ only
4.   $(B)$ and $(C)$ only
