1.   $\sin^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\right)$
2.   $\cos^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\right)$
3.   $\cos^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\right)$
4.   $\sin^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{6}}\right)$
