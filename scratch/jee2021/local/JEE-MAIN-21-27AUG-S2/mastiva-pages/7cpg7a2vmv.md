### Question 48

Physics · Motion in a Plane · Single correct

1.   $h_{max} = 10 \, \mathrm{m}$ $T = 2.5 \, \mathrm{s}$
2.   $h_{max} = 15.625 \, \mathrm{m}$ $T = 3.54 \, \mathrm{s}$
3.   $h_{max} = 15.625 \, \mathrm{m}$ $T = 1.77 \, \mathrm{s}$
4.   $h_{max} = 3.54 \, \mathrm{m}$ $T = 0.125 \, \mathrm{s}$
