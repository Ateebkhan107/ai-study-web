A player kicks a football with an initial speed of
25 ms” at an angle of 45° from the ground. What
are the maximum height and the time taken by the
football to reach at the highest point during
motion ? (Take g = 10 ms)

(1) Binax= 10 m T=2.5s

(2) bnax= 15.625m T=3.54s
(3) bmx = 15.625m T=1.77s

(4) Bmax = 3.54 m T=0.125s
