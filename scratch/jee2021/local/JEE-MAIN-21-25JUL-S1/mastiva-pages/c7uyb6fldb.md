Let $f : [0, \infty) \to [0, \infty)$ be defined as $$f(x) = \int_0^x [y] \, dy$$ where $[x]$ is the greatest integer less than or equal to $x$. Which of the following is true?

1.   $f$ is continuous at every point in $[0, \infty)$ and differentiable except at the integer points.
2.   $f$ is both continuous and differentiable except at the integer points in $[0, \infty)$.
3.   $f$ is continuous everywhere except at the integer points in $[0, \infty)$.
4.   $f$ is differentiable at every point in $[0, \infty)$. Official
