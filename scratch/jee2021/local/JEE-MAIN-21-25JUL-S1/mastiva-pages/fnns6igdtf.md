Reason : Aryl halides do not undergo nucleophilic substitution reaction. In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below:

1.   Both (A) and (R) true but (R) is not the correct explanation of (A).
2.   (A) is false but (R) is true.
3.   Both (A) and (R) true and (R) is correct explanation of (A).
4.   (A) is true but (R) is false.
