For extrinsic semiconductors; when doping
level is increased;

(1) Fermi-level of p-type semiconductor will go
upward and Fermi-level of n-type
semiconductors will go downward.

(2) Fermi-level of p-type semiconductors will
go downward and Fermi-level of n-type
semiconductor will go upward.

(3) Fermi-level of both p-type and n-type
semiconductros will go upward for T > Tp
K and downward for T < T; K, where Tp
is Fermi temperature.

(4) Fermi-level of p and n-type semiconductors
will not be affected.
