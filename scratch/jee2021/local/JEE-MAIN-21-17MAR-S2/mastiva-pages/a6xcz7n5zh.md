1.   monotonic on $(-\infty, 0) \cup (0, \infty)$
2.   not monotonic on $(-\infty, 0)$ and $(0, \infty)$
3.   monotonic on $(0, \infty)$ only
4.   monotonic on $(-\infty, 0)$ only
