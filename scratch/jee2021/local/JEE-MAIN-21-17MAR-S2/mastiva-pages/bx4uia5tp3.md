1.   $v_1^2 - v_2^2 = \frac{2h}{m} [f_1 - f_2]$

3.   $v_1 + v_2 = \left[ \frac{2h}{m} (f_1 + f_2) \right]^{-\frac{1}{2}}$
4.   $v_1 - v_2 = \left[ \frac{2h}{m} (f_1 - f_2) \right]^{1/2}$
