Let [t] denote the greatest integer less than or equal

to t. Let f(x) = x — [x], g(x) = 1 — x + [x], and

h(x) = min{f(x), g(x)}, x € [-2, 2]. Then h is :

(1) continuous in [—2, 2] but not differentiable at
more than four points in (—2, 2)

(2) not continuous at exactly three points in [—2, 2]

(3) continuous in [—2, 2] but not differentiable at
exactly three points in (—2, 2)

(4) not continuous at exactly four points in [—2, 2]
