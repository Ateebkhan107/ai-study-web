1.   $(-\infty, -1) \cup \left(\frac{1}{2}, \infty\right) - \{1\}$
2.   $(-\infty, \infty) - \{-1, 1\}$
3.   $(-1, \frac{1}{2}]$
4.   $(-\infty, \frac{1}{2}] - \{-1\}$
