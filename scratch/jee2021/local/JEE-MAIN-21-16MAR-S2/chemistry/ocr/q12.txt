12.

Ammonolysis of Alkyl halides followed by the
treatment with NaOH solution can be used to
prepare primary, secondary and tertiary
amines. The purpose of NaOH in the reaction
is :

(1) to remove basic impurities

(2) to activate NH; used in the reaction

(3) to remove acidic impurities

(4) to increase the reactivity of alkyl halide
