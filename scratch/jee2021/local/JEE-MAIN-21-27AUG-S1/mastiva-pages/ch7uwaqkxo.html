If for $x, y \in \mathbb{R}, x > 0$ $$y = \log_{10} x + \log_{10} x^{1/3} + \log_{10} x^{1/9} + \ldots upto \infty terms$$ and $$\frac{2 + 4 + 6 + \ldots + 2y}{3 + 6 + 9 + \ldots + 3y} = \frac{4}{\log_{10} x}$$, then the ordered pair $(x, y)$ is equal to:

  1. $(10^6, 6)$
  2. $(10^4, 6)$
  3. $(10^2, 3)$
  4. $(10^6, 9)$
