Let us consider a curve, $y = f(x)$ passing through the point $(-2, 2)$ and the slope of the tangent to the curve at any point $(x, f(x))$ is given by $f(x) + x f'(x) = x^2$ Then:

1.   $x^2 + 2x f(x) - 12 = 0$
2.   $x^3 + x f(x) + 12 = 0$
3.   $x^3 - 3x f(x) - 4 = 0$
4.   $x^2 + 2x f(x) + 4 = 0$
