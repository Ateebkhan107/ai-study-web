1.   $A_1 = A_2$ and $A_1 + A_2 = \sqrt{2}$

3.   $2 A_1 = A_2$ and $A_1 + A_2 = 1 + \sqrt{2}$
4.   $A_1 : A_2 = 1 : \sqrt{2}$ and $A_1 + A_2 = 1$
