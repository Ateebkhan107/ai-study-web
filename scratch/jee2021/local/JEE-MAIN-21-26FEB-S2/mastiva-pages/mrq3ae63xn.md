### Question 37

Physics · System of Particles and Rotational Motion · Single correct

The weight falls from rest. After falling through a distance 'h', the square of angular velocity of wheel will be:

1.   $\frac{2gh}{I+mr^2}$
2.   $2gh$
3.   $\frac{2mgh}{I+2mr^2}$
4.   $\frac{2mgh}{I+mr^2}$
