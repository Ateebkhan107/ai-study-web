### Question 47

Physics · Motion in a Straight Line · Single correct

1.   $\frac{a_1 + a_2}{a_2}$
2.   $\frac{a_2}{a_1}$
3.   $\frac{a_1 + a_2}{a_1}$
4.   $\frac{a_1}{a_2}$
