### Question 9

1.   $F_1$ is not a tautology but $F_2$ is a tautology
2.   $F_1$ is a tautology but $F_2$ is not a tautology
3.   $F_1$ and $F_2$ both are tautologies
4.   Both $F_1$ and $F_2$ are not tautologies
