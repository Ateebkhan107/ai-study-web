### Question 65

Chemistry · Surface Chemistry · Single correct

Given below are two statement : one is labelled as Assertion $\textbf{A}$ and the other is labelled as Reason $\textbf{R}$. Assertion $\textbf{A}$ : $\mathrm{SO_2 (g)}$ is adsorbed to a large extent than $\mathrm{H_2 (g)}$ on activated charcoal. Reason $\textbf{R}$ : $\mathrm{SO_2 (g)}$ has a higher critical temperature than $\mathrm{H_2 (g)}$. In the light of the above statements, choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below.

1.   Both A and R are correct but R is not the correct explanation fo A
2.   Both A and R are correct and R is the correct explanation of A.
3.   A is not correct but R is correct.
4.   A is correct but R is not correct.
