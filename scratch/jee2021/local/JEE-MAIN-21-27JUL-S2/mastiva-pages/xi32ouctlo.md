## JEE Main 27 July 2021 Shift 2 Question 5

### Question 5

Maths · Complex Numbers and Quadratic Equations · Single correct

Let $\mathbb{C}$ be the set of all complex numbers. Let $$S_1 = \{ z \in \mathbb{C} : |z - 2| \leq 1 \}$$ and $$S_2 = \{ z \in \mathbb{C} : z(1+i) + \overline{z}(1-i) \geq 4 \}.$$ Then, the maximum value of $$\left| z - \frac{5}{2} \right|^2$$ for $$z \in S_1 \cap S_2$$ is equal to:

1.   \frac{3+2\sqrt{2}}{4}
2.   \frac{5+2\sqrt{2}}{2}
3.   \frac{3+2\sqrt{2}}{2}
4.   \frac{5+2\sqrt{2}}{4}
