If total force of $4 \, \mathrm{N}$ is exerted on the dipole, find out the amount of positive or negative charge of the dipole.

1.   $815.1 \, \mathrm{nC}$
2.   $8.8 \, \mu \mathrm{C}$
3.   $0.485 \, \mathrm{mC}$
4.   $4.44 \, \mu \mathrm{C}$
